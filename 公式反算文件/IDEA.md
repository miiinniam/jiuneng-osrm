A reverse logistics cost calculator that derives optimal shipping rates from target product margins and market prices.
- Parse product SKU, selling price, and desired margin to compute maximum allowable shipping cost per region.
- Implement a carrier rate engine that selects the cheapest valid service from multiple provider APIs (e.g., SF Express, YTO) in real time.
- Visualize cost-to-serve heatmaps and suggest price adjustments when no carrier meets the margin threshold.
- Export batch-optimized rate tables for ERP/WMS integration, reducing manual freight negotiation time.
